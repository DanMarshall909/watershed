Watershed

Shape the land, steer the rain, and keep prey, hunters, foragers and scavengers alive through the wet and the dry. Water follows the ground and wears it away: rivers cut their valleys, lakes fill the hollows and slowly silt up, and the sun takes the water back. Illnesses come and go, droughts and floods arrive without warning, volcanoes wake, and newcomers wander in from the edges.
